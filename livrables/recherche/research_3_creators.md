## (b) Synthesis: the 18 most important opinions and tactics for 2025-2026

### Where the experts disagree with Google

1. **AI Max is not for small accounts yet.**
   - Google says: AI Max gives +7% conversions at similar CPA (#63) and is being auto-applied to campaigns with broad match or automatically created assets (#61).
   - Experts say:
     - Geddes sets prerequisites: 30+ conversions/month, profitable broad match, and no impression share lost to budget (#3).
     - Lolk says it is risky for small accounts and should be its own campaign type (#6, #8).
     - Independent data shows +13% value but +16% CPA, with only 22% of campaigns near target (#59, #60). One 4-month test found AI Max more than 2x as expensive as phrase match (#59).
   - Action: confirm that automatically created assets and campaign-level broad match are OFF, so the account was not silently upgraded on Sept 1, 2026 (#61, #62).

2. **Broad match: start exact, expand only when Search is maxed out.**
   - Google pushes broad match + Smart Bidding.
   - Hepp: start exact for local (#35, #36). Jyll: start new campaigns on exact match (#52) and broaden only once non-brand impression share is above 70% (#56). Lolk: avoid broad on small data (#8). Soares (French, #65) gives the same warning.
   - Exact and phrase match now reach AI Mode for explicit intent (#31, #32), which removes one of Google's main arguments for broad match.

3. **PMax for lead gen is the clearest disagreement.**
   - Search beat PMax on conversion rate 84% of the time on shared non-retail terms (#58). Optmyzr: Search converted 18.9% vs 6.2% for PMax, and PMax steals traffic even from exact match (#22).
   - Hepp calls PMax "optimized for Google" for local (#36). Geddes suggests pausing PMax for lead gen (#58). NAV43: 80/20 Search/PMax until CRM import is working (#64).
   - Jyll is more pro-PMax, but says never run lead-gen PMax on raw form fills (#12).
   - GML 2026 had zero PMax announcements but 42 lead-gen launches (#54).

4. **Ignore or disable auto-applied recommendations.**
   - "Remove redundant keywords" removes the protection exact match gives you against PMax (#22, #58).
   - Budget-increase suggestions mainly serve Google (#65). Google's own help docs have been wrong before (#24).
   - Brad's audit guide puts auto-created assets under human review (#57).

5. **Ad Strength is a vanity metric.** "Average" Ad Strength beat "Excellent" on CPA. Short sentence-case headlines and partial pinning win (#21, #26). Don't write ads to satisfy the Ad Strength meter.

6. **Distrust of Google's statements has grown.**
   - The Aug 17, 2026 target-bidding change was called a "cash grab" and raised CPCs about 20% even in campaigns outside its announced scope (#39, #43).
   - Jyll's advice: don't run tCPA chronically limited by budget. Use Max Conversions or raise the budget (#13).

7. **Negative keywords: experts split.**
   - Jyll and Fred say pruning legacy negatives can help Smart Bidding (#50, #55).
   - Lolk says manual negatives are essential for small accounts (#8). Levy says neglected negatives are a core problem (#26). Hepp and Brad agree with them.
   - For a small budget, keep negatives. Pruning is a large-account tactic.

### Consensus tactics (relevant to a small premium local lead-gen business)

8. **Lead quality comes from the signals you feed Google.**
   - Import qualified and booked leads through offline conversions or Enhanced Conversions for Leads (#12, #20, #64). Fred: "compete on signal quality" (#25).
   - Without this feedback, PMax and broad match optimize toward spam (#12, #64).

9. **Use a value or stage ladder.**
   - Track form fill, then qualified lead, then quote sent, then booked, with values worked back from the average sale value (#38).
   - Start on Max Conversions. Move to tCPA at 30-50 conversions/month. Demote early stages to secondary as volume grows (#55, #14).

10. **Small budget rules.**
    - At least 10 clicks/day per campaign (#15, #35). Few, consolidated campaigns (#8, #35, #37). Monthly or quarterly optimization cadence (#8).
    - Do nothing for 2-3 days after a change (#49).

11. **Higher CPCs are acceptable, junk traffic is not.** Max Conversions raised CPC from $1.77 to $10 while CPL fell from $121 to $68 (#14). For a premium brand, don't chase cheap clicks.

12. **Creative should filter leads.** Ad copy should attract the right buyers and repel poor-fit ones (#11, #12, #53). Mention price or minimums and location in RSAs, and test messages across ad groups (#4, #21).

13. **Local essentials.**
    - Presence-only location targeting (#35). Location assets linked to Google Business Profile, whose hours and distance pre-qualify leads (#34). Call assets (#35).
    - Tight geography: in Quebec, CPCs run $1-15 (#67).

14. **Competitor and brand safety.** Use brand exclusions and negatives for competitor names, since broad match and AI Max trigger ads on competitor brands (#10, #26, #30). If you do compete, use honest comparison pages (#10).

15. **Use first-party data.** Upload a past-client Customer Match list even below the targeting threshold, to improve bidding signals (#51). Use converting search terms to seed Demand Gen or YouTube audiences (#27).

16. **AI Overviews and AI Mode are shrinking clicks.**
    - Clicks are down 15-20% but the users who click convert better (#26). Lead gen loses more clicks than brick and mortar (#45). AI Overviews now appear on about 80% of branded queries (#47).
    - Budget for higher CPCs, and judge results on cost per qualified lead, not CTR (#25).

17. **Don't hand strategy to AI chatbots or agents.** An LLM-suggested bid-strategy change blew up a low-volume account (#9). Agents over-optimize (#17, #18). Use AI for drafting and analysis only (#41).

18. **Fix everything outside the ad account.** Landing page, speed and response time matter more than ad tweaks (#36, #40, #53). "A good campaign can still fail if the client can't close" (#44). In Quebec, Law 25 consent is required for remarketing cookies (#67, #68).

### Bottom line for an event-rental business in Quebec (premium, limited budget, wants fewer bad leads)

- **Campaigns:** one or two Search campaigns on exact and phrase match, presence-only targeting on the real service area, location and call assets, and strong account-level negatives (cheap, DIY, jobs, used/for sale).
- **AI Max, broad match and PMax:** keep AI Max off, and confirm automatically created assets and campaign-level broad match are off. No PMax until the account sees 30-50 qualified conversions/month with offline import.
- **Bidding:** Max Conversions on a "qualified lead" or "quote sent" action fed back from the CRM (or Google's new Leads in Google Ads tool). Keep raw form fills secondary.
- **Ads:** state price positioning and minimums in RSAs to filter bargain hunters. Ignore the Ad Strength meter.
- **Recommendations:** turn off auto-applied recommendations, especially "remove redundant keywords", broad-match upgrades and budget increases.
- **Cadence:** review monthly, not daily.
