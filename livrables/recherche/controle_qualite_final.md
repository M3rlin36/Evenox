## Québec-French typos / grammar / wording

No mechanical problems (no double spaces, mojibake, missing French spacing before `:`, or bad capitalization). Wording issues:

1. **"Facture nette 30 jours"** (callout, Corporatif & Fêtes): the wording is off. "Facture nette" means an invoice net of taxes or discounts. Use **"Paiement net 30 jours"** or the site's own wording, **"Facturation nette 30 jours"**. The site also says it applies only *"sur approbation de crédit"*, which the callout leaves out. "Bon de commande accepté" / "Purchase orders accepted" have the same condition.
2. **"Célébration 380 $ installé"** (Lettres lumineuses mariage): the agreement is ambiguous. The page says *"3 à 4 caractères · installés"*. Suggested: "Célébration 380 $, installées" or "Célébration 380 $ : installation".
3. **Missing "de" in keyword-style headlines**: "Location lettres lumineuses", "Location vidéobooth 360", "Location photobooth". The correct form is "Location de …". Acceptable as keyword-match headlines, but grammatically clipped.
4. **"Highlight livré le lendemain"**: anglicism. The site says "Montage highlight", so it is consistent, but "Vidéo-souvenir livrée le lendemain" would read more naturally.
5. **EN "One single supplier"**: redundant. Use "One supplier for everything" or "A single supplier".
6. **EN "Montréal, Laval and North Shore."** (Marquee D3): should be "Laval and **the** North Shore".
