## (b) Evenox site audit for Google Ads conversion (concrete fixes)

Strengths to exploit in ads: real price transparency on product pages (tents $300-$1,000, tables $10-15, photobooth from $599, packages from $899/$1,195) which most competitors hide; 4.8/5 (52 Google reviews); 1000+ events; blue-chip corporate logos; turnkey delivery+install; net-30 for corporate; 24h response; Sainte-Thérèse warehouse pickup with no minimum.

Weaknesses and fixes (priority order):
1. No dedicated ad landing pages per intent. Build 5 lean LPs (FR, EN mirror): Chapiteau, Tables & chaises, Photobooth, Party de bureau/Corporatif, Mariage décor. Each: H1 = keyword + zone ("Location de chapiteau Rive-Nord & Laval - installation incluse"), price-from above the fold, 3 packages, short form, reviews, logos, FAQ. Strip mega-menu from LPs.
2. Form friction and missing lead qualification. Current form requires full venue address. Change to: Type d'événement (select), Date, Ville (select or postal code), Nb d'invités (range), Nom, Courriel, Téléphone; make address optional. Add a 2-step layout (event details first, contact second). Add hidden fields for gclid/utm.
3. Two competing conversion paths (Booqable "Réserver" vs "Soumission"). For ads, pick one primary per LP: corporate/wedding = quote form; tables/chairs/tents small orders = configurator/Booqable. Track both as separate conversion actions (Booqable checkout via cross-domain tracking/linker; quote form submit; click-to-call >60s; phone from ads via call assets).
4. Booqable shop shows no prices on listing pages and is French-only. Enable price display on the grid; add an English locale or at least EN microcopy; make sure Google tag + linker is on evenox.booqableshop.com.
5. Inconsistent claims hurt trust and ad policy (misleading claims risk): "24h" vs "same-day"; "7/7 support" vs phone hours Mon-Fri 12-18; delivery minimum $200 (FAQ) vs $300 (team-building page); decor packages $295-$995 (city page) vs $899-$1,449 (forfaits page). Harmonize everywhere before launching ads.
6. Phone hours (weekday 12-18) mismatch when ads drive calls (weekday mornings are prime B2B time). Either extend to 9-18 or schedule call assets only during staffed hours and use a callback form otherwise.
7. Gmail business address (evenox.ca@gmail.com) reduces B2B trust. Use info@evenox.ca / soumission@evenox.ca.
8. Reviews missing on product pages (tables, inflatables, team building). Add Google review widget + 3 named testimonials per LP; add client logos to corporate LP.
9. Geographic overreach: Longueuil/Rive-Sud page while pricing logic is 40 km from Ste-Thérèse. In Ads, geo-target core radius (Rive-Nord, Laval, Montréal north/west, Terrebonne, Mirabel, St-Eustache) and run Rive-Sud/downtown only on corporate campaigns with "soumission personnalisée".
10. Missing urgency/seasonal messaging. Add dynamic banners: "Partys de bureau décembre: dates du vendredi presque complètes" (Oct-Nov); "Saison des mariages 2027: réservez votre chapiteau" (Jan-Apr).
11. Risk reversal is under-sold: 12-month credit on cancellation and free postponement are strong - surface them on LPs and as callout assets ("Report gratuit", "Annulation = crédit 12 mois").
12. Media: add a 20-40s setup video and real event photos (tents installed, corporate party) on LPs; reuse in PMax/YouTube.
13. Measurement: implement GA4 + Google Ads conversion tags via GTM, enhanced conversions for leads (hashed email), and later import "booking confirmed (deposit paid)" offline conversions with value so bidding optimizes on revenue, not raw leads.
14. Language/compliance: French-first ads and LPs (Bill 96); EN campaigns as separate ad groups pointing to EN pages (currently EN coverage is thin - a gap vs Diva/Celebrations/Louevie/Spiniko which are bilingual).
15. Brand visibility is weak (brand search returned no Evenox result in test engine). Run a small brand campaign + keep Google Business Profile updated (reviews from 52 toward 100+).
