# Evenox: Competitor ads, landing page/lead form CRO, Quebec French ad copy, and lead follow-up research

Prepared 2026-10-07. The scope is premium turnkey event equipment rental in Quebec's French market. Research on ChatGPT Ads is excluded because it is covered elsewhere.

## Method and limits
- **Google Ads:** I queried the Google Ads Transparency Center's internal RPC (SearchCreatives) by domain and advertiser ID, region Canada (2124). I pulled every creative for 20+ Quebec competitors and a few US and France brands, then read the rendered creatives (text, image and Maps ads). The Transparency Center's own counts are given below (n = number of creatives, first and last date shown).
- **Meta Ad Library:** The Meta Ad Library returned HTTP 403 and needs a logged-in session, so **I could not see any Meta ads**. Action item: check facebook.com/ads/library manually (Country = Canada, keywords "location chapiteau", "location mobilier", "party de bureau", plus each competitor's page name).
- **Prices:** Price signals come from competitors' public pages and directories. Most premium players hide their prices.

### 1d. Gaps Evenox can use
1. Nobody in Quebec search ads leads with **"clé en main + one invoice + delivery, installation and teardown included + guaranteed response time"** for **corporate** buyers. Competitors lead with products (chairs, tablecloths, glassware) or low price ("Prix Très Compétitifs", "20 % de rabais", "Aucun achat minimum").
2. Only DX shows corporate logos. Only Bright-style operators use review counts. **Diva and Festi-Fêtes have 4.5★ with 70–106 Google reviews.** Evenox needs a comparable review count (BrightLocal: 47% of consumers won't use a business with fewer than 20 reviews).
3. Nobody shows a winter or heated angle for the Fêtes except Grandchamp ("4 saisons chauffables") and Cité-Fêtes.
4. The regional markets (Gatineau, Sherbrooke, Trois-Rivières) have only generalists and no premium turnkey advertiser.
5. **Quebec wedding budgets are low:** median spend is $10k–$20k and 47% of couples spend under $10k (Wealthsimple Quebec sample). Premium wedding targeting should therefore be narrow (guest count 100+, venue type "domaine/grange/chapiteau privé"). The **corporate segment** (party des Fêtes, 5 à 7, product launches, galas) is the better premium source.

## 2. Landing page and lead form CRO for high-ticket event rental

### Benchmarks
- **Unbounce, Events & Leisure:** median landing page conversion is about 4.2%. The top quartile reaches 27.9%. Click-through CTA pages convert at 10.5% versus form pages. Pages with fewer than 200 words, simple language and emotional language do better. Only about 2.9% of **paid search** visitors submit forms on form pages.
- **Zuko form benchmarks:** view-to-completion is 37.2% on desktop and 31.3% on mobile. Starter-to-completion is 55.5% on desktop and 47.5% on mobile. Abandonment at the phone field is 6.3% and at the email field 6.4%.
- **Party rental PPC case (seoplus+, The Party Centre GTA):** +417% conversions and −82% CPA in year 1. A kids' events PPC case had a CPL of $29.47.

### Rules

| # | Rule | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Use a multi-step form. Put easy questions first (event type, date, guest count) and contact details last.** | Multi-step forms convert at about 13.9% versus 4.5% for single-page forms (Formstack). Venture Harbour went from 0.96% to 8.1%. BrokerNotes went from 11% to 46%. |
| 2 | **Don't just cut fields. Make each field feel justified.** | Imagescape went from 11 to 4 fields and saw +120%. But in an entertainment booking test (Aagaard/CXL), cutting from 9 to 6 fields gave −14%, while keeping 9 fields with better labels gave +19%. HubSpot found that textareas and multiple dropdowns hurt the most. |
| 3 | **Ask qualifying fields that filter out bad leads:** event type (corporate, wedding, private), date, city or venue, guest count, budget range (for example "< 2 500 $ / 2 500–5 000 / 5 000–10 000 / 10 000 $+"), and the service level wanted (livraison seule vs clé en main). | Wedding vendors reject 70–80% of inquiries because of budget or date. Filter on date and budget first. |
| 4 | **Show a "À partir de X $" price, or packages, instead of hiding prices.** | 78% of couples say price is the #1 factor in deciding whom to contact. Complete pricing profiles get about 40% more bookings. Showing rates upfront gave +25% response. |
| 5 | **Use three tiers (Essentiel / Signature / Prestige) and tag the middle one "le plus populaire".** | Anchoring and middle-option bias. About a 50% average-ticket lift when moving from single quotes to three-tier quotes. |
| 6 | **Offer an instant estimate or configurator, then capture contact details to send the detailed quote.** | Interactive calculators show about 40–60% conversion lift (vendor claims). Options.fr uses a configurator plus "devis sous 24h". |
| 7 | **Let people book a call right after submitting (calendar).** | Chili Piper customers: book rate up from 40% to 90%, and 2x inbound conversion. |
| 8 | **Put proof near the form:** Google rating and review count, corporate logos, a real-event gallery, and testimonials with name and company. | Reviews raise purchase likelihood by 270% (5 vs 0 reviews), and by 380% for high-priced items. 31% of consumers require 4.5★ or more. 74% only care about reviews from the last 3 months. Testimonials raise form submissions by up to 50%. |
| 9 | **Keep mobile load under 3 seconds and add click-to-call and SMS buttons.** | 53% of mobile visits are abandoned after 3 seconds. Bounce probability rises 32% going from 1 to 3 seconds. |
| 10 | **Write a benefit CTA:** "Recevoir ma soumission en 24 h" rather than "Envoyer". | 10–30% higher click rates than "Submit". |
| 11 | **Meta lead forms: use "Higher intent" (review screen) plus 2–3 qualifying questions.** | About 20–40% less volume, but it removes accidental taps. |
| 12 | **Track qualified leads and booked events as offline conversions, not raw form fills.** Add negative keywords such as gratuit, pas cher, emploi, usagé, à vendre and DIY. | Pinpoint Digital, and match-type guidance. |
| 13 | **Make every page legally compliant:** Loi 25 consent (separate, unchecked box, link to the privacy policy) and Charte de la langue française (French must be clearly predominant in ads). | CAI / Gowling / DLA Piper. |
| 14 | **Publish a minimum order and the service area**, as US premium operators do ($300–$1,500). | Folklore, Lost & Found, Social Llama. |

## 3. Quebec French ad copy

### Vocabulary
- **Use these search terms:** location chapiteau / tente de réception; location mobilier lounge; party de bureau; party des Fêtes; 5 à 7; événement corporatif; mariage; réception; soumission.
  - **soumission** is the usual Quebec word for a quote. Keep **devis** for France.
  - **clé en main** = turnkey.
  - **livraison, installation et démontage inclus** = delivery, installation and teardown included.
- **What OQLF prefers, and how to handle it:**
  - OQLF prefers "fête de bureau" over "party de bureau" (an anglicism), but people search for "party de bureau". Use the search term in keywords and headlines, and use "fête/soirée" in body copy for a premium tone.
  - OQLF prefers the spelling "cinq à sept", but "5 à 7" is the common search form.
  - OQLF prefers "haut de gamme" to "premium".
  - "Chapiteau" is the standard tent word in Quebec ads, used by every competitor.
- **Register:** Most competitors use "vous". DX deliberately uses "tu" ("fun fiable facile"). For a premium corporate buyer, **use "vous"**.

### Character limits
Responsive search ads allow up to 15 headlines of 30 characters and 4 descriptions of 90 characters. Pin headlines rarely.

### 10 headline patterns
1. "[Service] clé en main – [Ville]" → "Location clé en main à Laval"
2. Guaranteed response time → "Soumission détaillée en 24 h"
3. Inclusions → "Livraison + installation incluses"
4. Event type → "Party des Fêtes clé en main"
5. "5 à 7 corporatif sans tracas"
6. Capacity and spec (as competitors do) → "Chapiteaux jusqu'à 300 invités"
7. Proof → "4,9★ – [N] avis Google"
8. Price transparency → "Forfaits dès 1 500 $"
9. Risk reversal → "Prix fixe, aucune surprise"
10. Outcome or emotion → "Un décor qui impressionne"

**Extra patterns**
- Scarcity: "Dates de décembre limitées"
- Season: "Chapiteaux chauffés 4 saisons"
- Authority: "Ils nous font confiance : [logos]"
- Advisor: "Un conseiller dédié à votre événement" (the Atawa model)

### Descriptions (90 characters max)
- "Mobilier lounge, chapiteau, éclairage et décor : un seul fournisseur, une seule facture."
- "Laval, Montréal, Rive-Nord. Livraison, installation et démontage inclus. Soumission 24 h."

## 4. Lead follow-up speed and qualification

### Statistics
- **HBR 2011 (2,241 companies; 1.25M leads):**
  - Contacting a lead within 1 hour makes it nearly **7x** more likely to qualify than contacting it an hour later, and **60x** more likely than waiting 24 hours or more.
  - Only 37% of companies replied within 1 hour. 23% never replied. The average reply time was 42 hours.
- **MIT/InsideSales:** at 5 minutes versus 30 minutes, the lead is **100x** more likely to be contacted and **21x** more likely to be qualified.
- **Velocify (3.5M leads):** calling within 1 minute gives **+391%** conversion. 93% of converted leads are reached by the 6th call attempt.
- **Drift:** only 7% of B2B companies replied within 5 minutes. 55% had not replied after 5 business days.
- **WeddingPro / The Knot:**
  - Up to 50% of bookings go to the vendor who responds first.
  - A follow-up within 5 minutes gives 9x the conversion.
  - 1 in 2 couples expect a reply within 24 hours.
  - 7 in 10 say vendor responsiveness is the most important factor.
  - Keep replies under 200 words and end with one question.
  - Auto follow-ups get about 40% more responses.
- **The Bash:** the industry average reply time is 9 hours. Top performers reply in 6 hours or less.
- **HoneyBook:** reply within 30 minutes. 36% of clients leave because a business was slow or hard to reach.
- **SMS:** 98% open rate, about 90% read within 3 minutes. A text follow-up gives +112% engagement.

### Recommended sequence
| When | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|
| T+0 (auto) | SMS + email | "Bonjour [Prénom], ici [Nom] d'Évenox. Bien reçu votre demande pour le [date] à [ville] ([N] invités). Je vous appelle dans les prochaines minutes — ou réservez un appel ici : [lien]." |
| T+5 min | Phone | Qualification call (script below). If no answer, leave a voicemail and send an SMS. |
| T+2 h | Email | Quote or 3 package options (Essentiel / Signature / Prestige) with photos of similar events and the rating. |
| Day 1 | SMS | "Avez-vous pu regarder la proposition? Une question rapide : le lieu a-t-il un accès pour camion?" |
| Day 3 | Phone + email | Social proof (a corporate case study) and a check on the date's availability. |
| Day 5–7 | Email | Scarcity ("Nous retenons votre date jusqu'au [date]"), then close. |
| Day 14 / 30 | Email | Nurture: season guide, then a final check-in. |

**Qualification call script (5–7 min)**
1. Confirm the event type, date, city and venue (indoor or outdoor, truck access).
2. Ask about guest count and format (cocktail/5 à 7, seated dinner, gala).
3. Ask for the vision: "Quelle ambiance voulez-vous créer?"
4. Ask about budget: "Pour des événements similaires, nos clients investissent entre X et Y $ — est-ce dans votre ordre de grandeur?"
5. Ask who decides (an event committee, HR or a planner) and the decision deadline.
6. Agree the next step: send 3 options within 24 h, then hold a 15-minute review call.

**Compliance**
- **CASL:** an inquiry gives implied consent, so the follow-up is allowed, but get explicit consent before nurture marketing.
- **Loi 25:** use a separate, unchecked consent box.
