The closest categories to Evenox:
- **Arts & Entertainment (2026):** CPC $1.63, CTR 12.75%, CVR 5.91%, CPL $26.84 [BM]. This is a floor. It covers ticketed and entertainment businesses, not high-ticket B2B rental.
- **Furniture:** CVR 2.99%, CPL $106.70 (2026) [BM]
- **Industrial/Commercial services:** CPC $5.87, CVR 8.20%, CPL $75.19 (2026) [BM]
- **Restaurants/Hospitality:** CPC $2.05, CPL $30.57 [BM]

### Event and tent rental specifics (sparse; directional)
- "Tent rental" clicks cost **$2-6** depending on season (alwaysdobetter) [AG]
- QliqQliq's Canada study (Keyword Planner top-of-page bids, 100k+ keywords, 19 industries, Mar-Apr 2026) found that **event & party services averaged $3.33/click**, one of the cheapest verticals (insurance $33.81, legal $26.39) [AG-study]
- H1Site's Quebec CPCs in CAD: restaurants $1.50, e-commerce $2.50, dental $4.50, plumbing $6.00, insurance $12, legal $14. Agency fees in Montreal run 15-25% of spend [AG]
- Francophone markets are reported at 20-40% below US CPCs because auctions are thinner (Metricool/FrenchWeb snippet) [AG]
- Case study: The Party Centre (GTA tent and party rental) used Search + Display + SEO and got +417% conversions, +256% CVR and -82% CPA year over year. Spend and CPL were not disclosed [AG]
- Corporate-first structure, mobile bar hire (Sydney): CPL $18, 68% corporate clients. Remote team-building CPL $17.92 on $1,953/month [AG]

**Planning estimate for Evenox (inference):**
- Search CPC: CAD $2-5 for French wedding and party terms, CAD $4-8 for corporate and "chapiteau" terms
- CVR: 5-8% on a quote form
- CPL: **CAD $40-90** (wedding/private), **CAD $60-120** (corporate)

### Smart bidding and campaign-type minimums
- tCPA: requires at least 15 conversions in 30 days. Google recommends 30+ and evaluates over periods with at least 30 conversions [OFF/AG]
- Learning period: about 7 days, longer when conversion delays are long [AG]
- **Demand Gen:** Google best-practice docs say **$100+/day for Maximize** bidding, budget **at least 10x tCPA**, and "wait for 50 conversions" [OFF]. Google's Demand Gen PM said "$100 a day... bare minimum" (Ads Decoded, 1 Oct 2026) [NEWS/OFF]. The implementation guide cites 15-20x tCPA and 50 conversions per arm [OFF]
- **AI Max for Search:** $50/day minimum, Enhanced Conversions/OCI and DDA required [NEWS]
- **PMax for lead gen:** daily budget of at least 3x CPA, 30+ conversions/month, and offline conversion import (OCI) to avoid spam leads [AG]. At $100 CPA you need about $3k/month [AG]
- Demand Gen median B2B CPL $96 vs PMax $113 (small B2B SaaS sample, NA) [BM-small]
- YouTube in Canada: CPV CAD $0.15-0.40 (Wisevu) [AG]. Canadian YouTube CPM is among the highest globally (one study: $29) [AG]
- Local Services Ads: event/party rental **not an eligible category in Canada** [AG]
- Enhanced Conversions for Leads / OCI: uploads move to Data Manager API from 15 June 2026 [OFF]

### Seasonality (Quebec)
- ISQ: 23,049 marriages in 2024. **57% fall June-September.** Peak day was 24 Aug (742 ceremonies), and Saturdays dominate [OFF]
- Engagement season runs Nov-Feb (about 40% of proposals; December about 21%). Booking season runs Dec-May. Inquiries peak Dec-Feb (WeddingPro) [BM/AG]
- Canadian couples plan for about 14 months; average guest count is 154 [NEWS]
- Corporate holiday parties: venues and entertainment for December book in **Aug-Sept**, and options narrow by October. Corporate rentals typically book 2-3 months ahead [AG]
- Montreal business events (2025): 477 events, more than 1M visitors, $438M in spinoffs. Montreal has been #1 in North America for association congresses (ICCA) for 9 years [OFF]
- Summer festival season runs Jun-Aug (Francos, Jazz, JFL, etc.) [OFF]
- Meta CPMs in Canada bottom out in Jan and Sep and peak in Dec [BM]

## 2. Meta (Facebook/Instagram)

| Metric | Value | Reliability |
|---|---|---|

| Personal Services CPL | $30.57 | WordStream [BM] |

| Home Improvement CPL | $41.26 | WordStream [BM] |
| Lead campaign CTR / CPC / form CVR | 2.59% / $1.92 / 7.72% | AdAdvisor (WordStream) [BM] |
| Focus Digital NA CPL (hospitality / home services) | $37.20 / $45.50; average $42 | 138 campaigns [BM] |
| Wedding venues FB spend | $4-8k/month, usually with Google at a 70-80 / 20-30 split | clicksgeek [AG] |

- **Learning phase:** about **50 optimization events per ad set per 7 days**. Edits of more than about 20% reset it [OFF via agencies]. At a CAD $40 CPL, one ad set needs about CAD $2,000/week to exit learning. Evenox's whole Meta budget will not reach that, so expect "Learning Limited." Consolidate to 1 campaign with 1-2 ad sets.
- **Instant forms vs landing page:** Jon Loomer's test found instant-form CPL $2.04 vs $3.77 for the landing page, with near-identical quality (29.1% vs 29.0%) [AG-test]. For offers worth more than about £500 LTV, agencies report that landing pages raise CPL 30-40% but can halve the cost per qualified appointment [AG]. Use Higher-Intent forms with qualifying questions.
- Meta reports these results from its own data [OFF via NEWS]:
  - Advantage+ leads: -14% CPL and -10% cost per qualified lead (CPQL)
  - Conversion Leads optimisation with CRM data sent through Conversions API (CAPI): about 15-21% lower cost per quality lead
  - Offline Conversions API retired May 2025
- Detailed-targeting exclusions were removed in Jan 2025. Broad targeting plus Advantage+ audience is the default; use custom-audience exclusions only [NEWS]
- Creative for premium: real setup portfolio, drone shots, before/after "transformation" Reels under 30s, carousels of finished events, behind-the-scenes setup [AG]. Casual UGC can hurt luxury positioning; polished but native-looking content works better [AG]
- FB language delivery depends on interface, profile language and engagement, so French-only delivery cannot be guaranteed. Run French creative as the primary ad [AG, 2018]

  - Microsoft Ads LinkedIn profile targeting on search

## 5. Budget frameworks and campaign count
- At a $300/day budget, every additional campaign splits the conversion signal. Hagakure/consolidation logic: fewer campaigns, each with enough weekly conversions [AG/OFF]
- 70/20/10 rule for proven vs. growth vs. test spend [AG]. Revenue-backed budget = target revenue / ROAS [AG]
- Minimum budget per campaign:
  - Google Search tCPA: about 30 conversions/month, so about CAD $1,800-2,700/month at a $60-90 CPL
  - Meta: cannot reach 50/week; optimise for leads in one consolidated campaign of at least CAD $2,000-2,700/month
  - Demand Gen: at least $100/day (≈ CAD $4,000/month) and PMax: at least 3x CPA/day. **Neither fits at launch.**

### Recommended structure (max 4 concurrent paid campaigns, plus optional brand)
| # | Campaign | CAD/day | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google Search: Mariages & événements privés (FR primary, EN ad group) | ~$105 | 35% |
| 2 | Google Search: Corporatif / B2B (location matériel événementiel entreprise, party de Noël, chapiteau corporatif) | ~$75 | 25% |
| 3 | Meta Leads, one CBO campaign (prospecting + retargeting ad sets, Higher-Intent instant form or landing page, CAPI) | ~$90 | 30% |
| 4 | Microsoft Ads Search (Google import + LinkedIn profile bid modifiers) | ~$20 | 7% |
| (opt.) | Brand search | ~$10 | 3% |

Seasonal shifts within the same campaigns:
- Corporate weight goes up in Aug-Nov
- Wedding weight goes up in Dec-Mar and Apr-Jun
- Add PMax or Demand Gen only after Search reaches 30+ qualified conversions/month with offline conversion import running

## 6. Quebec rules
- **Charter of the French language / Bill 96:**
  - Commercial advertising, websites and social posts aimed at Quebec must be in French. Another language is allowed only if the French version is at least equivalent.
  - On signage and in ads where both languages share the same visual field, French must be "markedly predominant" (about twice the space) as of **1 June 2025**.
  - A recognised trademark may stay non-French only if no French version of it is registered.
  - Fines are **$3,000-30,000 per offence per day**, doubled for a second offence and tripled after that.
  - OQLF has fined English-only social ads ($1,500 real-estate broker case).
  - Practical rule: French ads and French landing pages by default. English ads only as a parallel version of equivalent quality.
- **Law 25:**
  - Opt-in consent for any tracking that identifies, profiles or locates (analytics, pixels, ad cookies). These must be off by default.
  - The banner needs "Refuse all" as prominent as "Accept all" and granular purposes; keep consent records.
  - Penalties reach $25M or 4% of worldwide turnover.
  - Implement a CMP with **Google Consent Mode v2 (Advanced)** so cookieless pings allow modeled conversions.
  - Gate Meta Pixel and Microsoft UET behind consent.
  - Rely on server-side signals (Enhanced Conversions for Leads, Meta CAPI for CRM, Microsoft offline conversions) to recover lost signal.
