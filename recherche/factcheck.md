Method: WebSearch/WebFetch only. help.openai.com, openai.com, ads.openai.com (Cloudflare), web.archive.org, jonloomer.com and legisquebec returned 403 or were blocked, so for OpenAI help-center pages I used search-engine extracts of the exact URLs (marked [S]). developers.openai.com, Google, Microsoft Learn, Statcounter, ISQ PDFs and evenox.ca were fetched directly (marked [F]). The ISQ PDFs and the evenox.ca pages were downloaded and parsed with curl.

## Notes on the conflicts
- **$50k minimum (research_paid_media.md #91, TechWyse):** this describes the managed-buy model of Feb–Apr 2026 and has been superseded by self-serve (US May 5; Canada listed on the availability page by mid-2026). Discard it.
- **Country-only versus province targeting:** both statements were true at different times. Province-level targeting is the current state. City targeting in Canada is not available.
- **Text customization default:** OpenAI's public wording is "opt in", while practitioners report the toggle defaults to on in Ads Manager. Treat it as on until you have checked each campaign. This matters for Bill 96, because auto-translated English variants could serve to Quebec users.
- **OpenAI Pixel consent defaults to true.** For Law 25, `oaiq("consent", false)` must run before init, or the pixel must load only after opt-in through the CMP.
